## Style Prompt

Clean student portfolio product demo for "片前助手", an AI pre-editing assistant for beginner short-video creators. The video should feel like a calm desk-and-screen walkthrough: warm off-white paper texture, restrained UI cards, soft shadows, notebook-like labels, and measured product motion. It should avoid sci-fi, cyberpunk, neon, and overpromising AI language.

## Colors

- Background paper: #f4f0e8
- Main paper card: #fffdf8
- Ink: #181614
- Muted text: #6d665d
- Line: #d8d0c5
- Deep blue accent: #315f8c
- Clay accent: #b8624f
- Soft green status: #5e7d63

## Typography

- Primary: Arial, "Microsoft YaHei", "PingFang SC", sans-serif
- Tone: portfolio case-study, compact UI labels, natural Chinese copy

## What NOT to Do

- No cyberpunk, neon glow, futuristic HUD, or dramatic tech gradients.
- Do not say the product makes a finished video automatically.
- Avoid generic AI slogans; keep copy close to creator workflow.
- Keep UI readable and calm, like a screen recording polished for a portfolio.
